— Seller Questions —

Straight answers before you list

The things Corpus Christi sellers ask Joe most. Have another? Just call or text.

What commission do I pay to sell my home?

Joe walks you through the full commission structure up front — no surprises. In Texas commissions are negotiable and agreed to in writing before your home ever hits the market. Ask Joe for a straight breakdown for your specific home and price point when you call for your free valuation.

How long does it take to sell a house in Corpus Christi?

It depends on your price, condition, and how the market is moving in your neighborhood. A home priced right and prepped well typically moves faster than one that sits overpriced. Joe gives you an honest read on current timelines for your area during your valuation — not a sales pitch.

Do I need to make repairs before I list?

Not always. Because Joe is a Realtor, investor, and contractor, he can tell you which repairs actually add value and which ones you can skip. If a few targeted fixes will raise your sale price, he'll say so — and if selling as-is makes more sense, he'll tell you that too.

How much is my home worth?

Your home's value comes from recent comparable sales, its condition, and current demand in your neighborhood. Joe gives you a free, no-obligation valuation based on real local data — not an automated online estimate. Call or text (361) 960-1779 to get yours.

Should I list on the market or take a cash offer?

Listing usually nets the most money when your home is ready to show. A cash offer can make more sense when you need speed, want to skip repairs and showings, or are selling an inherited or distressed property. Joe does both, so he'll lay out the numbers on each and let you decide with no pressure.
