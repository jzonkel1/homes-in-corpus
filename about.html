— Who Is Joe

Corpus Christi was his favorite place. He made it home.

Joe Monceballez is originally from New Braunfels. About 19 years ago he moved to Corpus Christi because it was his favorite place on earth — and he's built his whole career here, helping other people put down roots too.

Today he wears three hats: a licensed Realtor with ValTex Realty, an active real estate investor, and the general contractor on his own renovation projects. Very few people in Corpus Christi do all three.

He's closed 99 homes, and the houses he buys, he rebuilds himself. So when Joe tells you what a house is worth — or what a repair really costs — it isn't a guess. It comes from real trade experience.

Joe works in English and Spanish, he's licensed under TREC #0586828, and he makes himself available around the clock — because the best deals don't wait for business hours.
