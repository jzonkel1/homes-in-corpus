— Common Questions —

Answers before you call

What does it cost to work with Joe?

If you're buying, the seller typically pays the buyer's agent — so Joe's help usually costs you nothing. If you're selling, the commission is agreed on upfront before you list, with no surprises.

How fast can you sell my house?

It depends on price and condition. Priced right, Corpus Christi homes often sell in weeks. If you need out fast, Joe can make a direct cash offer and close on your timeline.

Should I list it or take a cash offer?

Listing on the market usually nets the most money. A cash offer is fastest and skips repairs and showings. Joe runs both numbers and tells you straight which one puts more in your pocket.

What areas does Joe cover?

Corpus Christi, Portland, Calallen, Flour Bluff, Rockport, Ingleside, Robstown, Aransas Pass, and North Padre Island.
