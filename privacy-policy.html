Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 30, 2026

Introduction

Joe Monceballez ("Joe," "we," "us," or "our"), a Texas real estate agent with ValTex Realty in Corpus Christi, TX, respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, and protected when you visit homesincorpus.com and use the contact forms on this site.

Information We Collect

We collect information you voluntarily provide through the contact and lead forms on this site, which may include your name, phone number, email address, property address, and any details you include in your message. We do not require you to create an account to use this site.

How We Use Your Information

Information you submit is used solely to respond to your inquiry — for example, to discuss listing your home, preparing a cash offer, setting up a home search, or answering your questions. By submitting a form, you agree that Joe may call, text, or email you back at the contact information you provided. We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for their marketing purposes.

Third-Party Services

This site uses a small number of third-party services to function: FormSubmit processes contact form submissions and delivers them to Joe's email; Google Fonts and Google Maps provide typography and the service-area map; the live listings feed is embedded from the Coastal Bend MLS (Matrix); and a promotional video is embedded from Facebook. These providers may receive standard technical data (such as your IP address) when their content loads. We encourage you to review their respective privacy policies.

Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, use, or disclosure. However, no method of internet transmission is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Cookies

This site stores a single preference in your browser (your language choice, English or Spanish). Embedded third-party content such as Google Maps, the MLS listings feed, and Facebook video may set their own cookies subject to their own policies.

Children's Privacy

This site is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of the personal information we hold about you, and you may opt out of future communications at any time — just say so by phone, text, or email.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date above reflects the most recent revision.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Contact Joe Monceballez at (361) 960-1779 or joe@homesincorpus.com.
