— Take a Deep Breath —

The worries almost every first-timer has

You're not the first person to feel this way, and none of it is a dealbreaker. Here's the honest reassurance.

Down Payment

"I don't have a huge down payment."

You may not need one as big as you think. Different loans have very different requirements, and there are programs designed to help buyers get in with less. Joe's lenders review your situation and walk you through the real options — no guessing.

Credit

"My credit isn't perfect."

You don't need perfect credit to START the conversation. A lender can tell you exactly where you stand and what's realistic today — and sometimes a few small steps make a real difference. The worst thing you can do is assume the answer is no without asking.

— First-Timer FAQ —

Straight answers to common questions

How much do I need for a down payment?

It depends on the loan you qualify for — there's no single number that fits everyone. Some loan types ask for a larger down payment and some ask for very little. Joe connects you with trusted local lenders who look at your situation and walk you through the real options before you ever tour a home, so you know your numbers up front.

Does using Joe cost me anything?

As your buyer's agent, Joe's commission costs you $0 — it's typically paid out of the transaction by the seller's side, not out of your pocket. You get a full-time Realtor, investor, and licensed contractor in your corner at no cost to you as the buyer.

What credit score do I need?

You don't need perfect credit to START the conversation. Different loans have different requirements, and Joe's lenders can review where you stand and tell you honestly what's realistic today and what small steps might help. The only way to know is to ask — that first chat is free and no-pressure.

How long does it take?

Every buyer moves at their own pace. Some are ready in a few weeks; others take months to get pre-approved and find the right place — and that's completely fine. Joe never rushes you. You go as fast or as slow as you're comfortable with.

Do you speak Spanish?

Yes — Joe is bilingual and helps buyers in English or Spanish. Se habla español. Every step of the process can be explained in the language you're most comfortable with.
